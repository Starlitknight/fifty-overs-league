--- THE ANSWER ---
  win-points per BAT  score-point : 0.3467
  win-points per BOWL score-point : 0.3143
  ratio (bowl / bat)              : 0.906

  A ratio of 1.0 means the two channels are already on one scale and the
  residuals are something else. A ratio above 1 means a bowling point buys
  MORE cricket than a batting point, so the coach is over-paying batting -
  which is what would make an all-rounder's batting look too good.
  The correction is to multiply the BAT channel by (bat slope / bowl slope),
  i.e. 1.103, or equivalently scale bowling up by 0.906.

  Below 1.0 means the model pays for lower-order batting at a rate the
  cricket does not, and SLOT_BALLS is too fat down the order - which would
  over-value every all-rounder, because an all-rounder always bats there.
