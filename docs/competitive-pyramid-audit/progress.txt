      P10 23.0   P25 35.0   median 51.0   P75 67.0   P90 80.0   max 93.0

      OVR 50+ : 52.2% of the board (313 of 600)
      OVR 56+ : 42.8% of the board (257 of 600)
      OVR 60+ : 35.7% of the board (214 of 600)
      OVR 67+ : 25.8% of the board (155 of 600)
      OVR 70+ : 22.8% of the board (137 of 600)

   A bottom club's worst man is around OVR 30 and its best around 50. Every
   free agent above its own worst man is an upgrade IT CAN MAKE, so supply
   is not obviously the binding constraint - the question the sections above
   answer is whether taking those upgrades actually moves the best XI.
