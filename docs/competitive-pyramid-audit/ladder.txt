6 + 5. WHAT A CARD GAP COSTS ON THE FIELD
============================================================================================

   Real matches through the shipped engine. Both sides are the SAME dealt
   squad put on a chosen overall by the engine's own fitToOvr - a similarity
   transform, so shape and roles survive and only the level differs. That
   isolates quality from squad shape, which a tier-vs-tier match cannot.

   a side at each level against a side at 48 (the d2a rung), 200 matches each:

      level   gap    win%   pts/14 rounds   what that is

         40    -8    12.5           3.5   effectively out of it
