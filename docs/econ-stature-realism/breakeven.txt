=== WHY: THE COST FLOOR A SMALL CLUB CANNOT MOVE ===

  Club operations are charged per round as
    $58,000 + seats x $3.1 (+ the top-flight premium)
  which is a cost of BEING a club, and it does not scale down with a
  club's commercial size. For the bottom of division two:
