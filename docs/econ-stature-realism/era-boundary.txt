=== THE GATE THAT WOULD BE NEEDED ===

  econStature(slot, isBoss) is called from six places. Only ONE of them
  is era-2-only, and it is the one that already takes the era:

    foundingBankFor(slot, isBoss, era2)   <- already branches on the era
    foundingBank(slot, isBoss)            era 1  -- MUST NOT MOVE
    foundingSeats(slot, isBoss)           both   -- MUST NOT MOVE for era-1 clubs
    foundingSupport(slot, isBoss)         both   -- MUST NOT MOVE for era-1 clubs
    sponsorSeasonValue(...)               both   -- era-1 sponsor is priced
                                                    WITHOUT stature (the era-1
                                                    branch calls sponsorOf, not
                                                    sponsorSeasonValue)
    ambitionOf(slot, isBoss)              bots; botMoney short-circuits in era 1

  So a correct implementation adds the era to the coordinate itself -
  econStature(slot, isBoss, era2) - and returns the CURRENT law whenever
  era2 is false. Every era-1 call site keeps the number it has today, by
  construction rather than by inspection.

  A club founded in era 1 that plays on into era 2 is the case to think
  hardest about: its founding capital is an era-1 fact and must stay put,
  while the sponsor it signs for an era-2 season is an era-2 fact. The
  seam is the SEASON, not the club, and economy.mjs already carries
  exactly that distinction (foundedEra2 for the founding line, curEra2 for
  the season being walked).
