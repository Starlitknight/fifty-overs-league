5. CAN IT BE EXPLOITED?
============================================================================================

   poor recruits at or above the free-agent MEDIAN: 0 of 1200 (0.0%)
   poor recruits at OVR 50 or better:               0 of 1200 (0.0%)
   most valuable poor recruit drawn:                $20,500
   median free-agent value:                         $422,250

   AGAINST THE CHEAPEST REALISTIC NORMAL REPLACEMENT (worst decile of board):
      cheapest normal    median OVR 17   wage $400   value $20,500
      emergency man      median OVR 4   wage $400   value $20,500
      P(emergency beats even the cheapest normal man) = 4.5%
      median experience  emergency 7 vs board 61

      A manager choosing between them is choosing between a man worse than
      the worst thing on the board and one who is not. There is no version
      of "let the squad shrink" that pays.

   WHAT KIND OF OBJECT HE IS, which matters as much as how good he is:
      colt flag          true
      age range          16 to 20
      carries yseed      yes - a hidden growth seed
      pid prefix         y (academy ids start "y")

   makeRecruit is the ACADEMY's primitive: every man it returns is a colt of
   sixteen to twenty carrying a hidden growth rate, which is exactly right
   for a youth intake and is a question mark for a SENIOR emergency signing.
   A colt in the senior squad trains at the senior rate - living.mjs runs the
   men and the boys as separate crews and only the boys crew is handed
   __ypot - so the growth seed is inert there. The flag itself is the risk:
   anything that reads `colt` to mean "this man is in the academy" would be
   wrong about him.
