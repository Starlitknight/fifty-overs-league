READ IT LIKE THIS. A positive edge means the coach's side really was the
better one. The bias question is whether the three rows DISAGREE: if the
coach over-values all-rounders, then "specialist -> all-rounder" (where it
kept the specialist) would look good while the two "all-rounder -> ..." rows
(where it kept the all-rounder) would look bad. Symmetric rows mean no
systematic role bias, whatever the overall accuracy.
