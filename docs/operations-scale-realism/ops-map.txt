CLUB OPERATIONS - THE EXACT LAW AND THE LADDER IT IS CHARGED ON
==============================================================================

1. THE FORMULA, verbatim from server/financeconfig.mjs:267

   operationsPerRound(seats, div, natOps) =
     round( (OPS_BASE_ROUND + seats x OPS_PER_SEAT_ROUND
             + (div === 1 ? OPS_TOPFLIGHT_ROUND : 0)) x natOps )

   OPS_BASE_ROUND       $58,000   every club, every round it plays
   OPS_PER_SEAT_ROUND   $3.1        per seat of CAPACITY (not of crowd)
   OPS_TOPFLIGHT_ROUND  $30,000   Division One only
   OPS_ASSOC            0.88        associate nations, applied to the WHOLE line

   No cap. No floor. No academy term (academy upkeep is its own ledger
   line, charged beside operations and not part of it). Charged once per
   round the club PLAYS - home and away alike, play-off rounds included
   since Phase 2.

3. WHAT THE LADDER SPANS, top to bottom

   seats            29,000 -> 24,000   1.208x
   supporters       24,240 -> 14,520   1.669x
   econStature      1.000 -> 0.620     1.613x
   ops/round        $177,900 -> $132,400   1.344x   (with the division premium)
   ops, no premium  $147,900 -> $132,400   1.117x   <- the CLUB-SCALE half alone

   Seats are IDENTICAL for slots 7-15 (9 of 16 clubs). For those seats the
   per-seat term is not a size term at all: it is a second fixed charge of $74,400.

   Effective fixed cost by that reading (base + an unvarying ground term):
     slot  0  ops  $177,900   varies with own size:  $15,500   =   8.7% of its operations
     slot  4  ops  $168,600   varies with own size:   $6,200   =   3.7% of its operations
     slot  7  ops  $162,400   varies with own size:       $0   =   0.0% of its operations
     slot  8  ops  $132,400   varies with own size:       $0   =   0.0% of its operations
     slot 15  ops  $132,400   varies with own size:       $0   =   0.0% of its operations
