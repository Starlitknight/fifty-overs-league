
1. WHAT A WAGE IS IN THIS GAME
======================================================================================

   Not A (a persistent contract salary) and not C (an abstract roster-cost
   proxy). It is B: A DYNAMICALLY DERIVED MARKET-RATE SALARY, and the
   architecture is unambiguous about it.

   server/living.mjs calls host.derive(squad) for every club on every settle
   and copies the derived fields back onto the player row. `wage` is in that
   list beside `rating` and `bat`. The engine's jsDerive ends:

       p.rating = Math.round(foOvr(p) * 1000);
       p.wage   = foWageOf(p.rating, (p.talents||[]).length, 1);

   So a man's wage is a pure function of his CURRENT card, recomputed three
   times an hour. Nothing negotiates it, nothing signs it, and it has no
   term. A cricketer who trains gets a rise the same week; a cricketer who
   declines takes a cut. There is no contract anywhere in the schema.

   THE ONE PLACE A WAGE IS REMEMBERED is the round it was charged in:
   migration 101 banks the bill per club per round in wage_rounds, and
   economy.mjs charges `billAt[...] ?? c.wages`. That is a LEDGER record of
   what was paid, not a contract - it stops history being restated, and it
   does not stop the next round being charged at a new price.

   This matters for the whole phase: a wage that re-derives cannot be
   changed "for new signings only". There are no old signings.

2. THE EXACT FORMULA
======================================================================================

   foWageOf(rating, talents, scar) =
       max(400, round( MID x (rating/R50)^K x (1 + 0.06 x talents) x scar / 10) x 10)

   read from engine/src/00-core.js:
      FO_WAGE_OVR50   50        the median professional, by the ladder's meaning
      FO_WAGE_R50     50000     ...as a rating, which is the card x 1000
      FO_WAGE_MID     9290      what that median man earns a round
      FO_WAGE_K       3       how fast the price of quality climbs

   what each parameter controls:
      R50    THE MIDPOINT'S LOCATION on the card scale. Not a level: moving
             it rescales every wage by (old/new)^K without changing any
             ratio. This is the constant that went stale once - see below.
      MID    THE ABSOLUTE LEVEL, and only that. Scaling it scales every wage
             in the world by the same factor and leaves every ratio alone.
      K      THE SHAPE, and only that. It tilts the ladder about OVR 50: a
             man above the median gets cheaper as K falls, a man below it
             gets DEARER. It cannot move the median man at all.
      400    a floor, in dollars, on what any professional is paid.
      0.06   a talent premium, multiplicative - worth more on a better man.

   the ladder, at the shipped constants:

      OVR    wage a round   vs median   +5 OVR    +10 OVR
       20           $590       0.06x      97%       241%
       30         $2,010       0.22x      59%       137%
       40         $4,760       0.51x      42%        95%
       50         $9,290       1.00x      33%        73%
       60        $16,050       1.73x      27%        59%
       70        $25,490       2.74x      23%        49%
       80        $38,050       4.10x      20%        42%
       90        $54,180       5.83x      18%        37%
       95        $63,720       6.86x      17%          -
      100        $74,320       8.00x        -          -

   SECTION 10: a +5 is worth 33% at OVR 50 and 17% at OVR 90; a +10 is 73%
   and 37%. The PREMIUM IN DOLLARS grows with quality while the premium in
   PERCENT shrinks, which is the right shape for a power law and is why a
   star costs a squad rather than a line item. Nothing here demands a linear
   relationship to measured cricket value, and the brief does not ask for one.

3. WHERE THE LAW LIVES - FOUR COPIES, AND THEY MUST MOVE TOGETHER
======================================================================================

   CANONICAL
      engine/src/00-core.js   foWageOf()          the authority

   MIRRORS, each with the constants written out because they cannot import
      server/market.mjs       wageFromRating()    the umpire's valuations
      engine 55-market.js     qsPrice()           what the page promises
      migrations 065 + 098    plpgsql             what MOVES THE MONEY on a
                                                  quicksell

   Migration 065 says so itself: "THE THREE PLACES THIS SUM LIVES must agree,
   or a manager is quoted one price and paid another... If any of the four
   move, all four move."

   AND MIGRATIONS ARE IMMUTABLE. 065 and 098 have run in production and
   cannot be edited; a wage change needs a NEW numbered migration to
   redefine the function. That is a cost of any change here, not a blocker.

   the mirror currently agrees with the engine, checked on REAL dealt men:
      OVR  84  0 talent(s)   engine    $44,050   market.mjs    $44,050   agree
      OVR  81  0 talent(s)   engine    $39,500   market.mjs    $39,500   agree

   DOWNSTREAM OF THE WAGE, everything that prices a man:
      market.mjs rawWorth   = wage x 18 x 2.4 x ageCurve x form     -> every fee

      botfinance botMoney   = squad wage bill                     -> bot posture
      economy.mjs           = sum of p.wage over squad + youth    -> the ledger
      squadStrength / UI    read the card, not the wage

18. PROMOTION AND RELEGATION UNDER EACH CANDIDATE
======================================================================================

   The SAME club, the SAME squad, the SAME following and the SAME finish,
   playing one season in Division One instead of Division Two. Promotion is
   measured BEFORE any discretionary strengthening, which is the guard.

   ON FOUNDING COORDINATES, which is a different basis from the $1,899,801
   Phase 4 reported: that figure came off the moving pyramid, where a
   promoted club carries the following it has EARNED rather than the one it
   was dealt. The level differs between the two bases; what matters here is
   that the premium does not move with the wage scalar, and it does not on
   either.

   slot 8, dealt bill $164,670 a round

    1.00     $128,445   $2,511,951    $2,383,506  -$2,383,506

    0.75     $711,954   $3,088,289    $2,376,335  -$2,376,335

   Promotion stays positive and relegation stays negative at every candidate,
   and by the SAME amount: the promotion premium is media, sponsor and gate
   against a division cost, and a wage scalar touches none of them. The
   pyramid guard is untouched by anything this phase could do.
