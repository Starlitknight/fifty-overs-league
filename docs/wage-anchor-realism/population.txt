9. SCALE vs SHAPE - the ladder of relative price

      ratio        value    what it means
      W95/W85        1.396    (95/85)^K, K=3
      W90/W80        1.424    (90/80)^K, K=3
      W80/W70        1.493    (80/70)^K, K=3
      W70/W60        1.588    (70/60)^K, K=3
      W60/W50        1.728    (60/50)^K, K=3
      W50/W40        1.953    (50/40)^K, K=3

   These are FIXED BY K AND NOTHING ELSE. foWageOf is FO_WAGE_MID x
   (card/50)^3, so every ratio between two cards is (a/b)^3 whatever the
   midpoint is. An absolute re-anchor cannot change one of them, and no
   measurement of the population can either. That is what makes SCALE and
   SHAPE genuinely separable here: FO_WAGE_MID moves the level, K moves the
   ladder, and they do not interact.
