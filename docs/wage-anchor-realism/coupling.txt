
16. THE TRANSFER MARKET IS PRICED IN WAGES
========================================================================================

   market.mjs rawWorth(p) = wage x 18 x 2.4 x ageCurve(age) x form

   The wage enters LINEARLY and nothing else in that expression knows about
   the anchor. So a scalar s on FO_WAGE_MID multiplies every fee, every
   quicksell and every bot valuation in the world by exactly s. There is no
   second-order term to discover and no way to change one without the other.

   Every column is the one beside it times the scalar, to the rounding. A
   re-anchor does not change what a player is worth RELATIVE to another
   player - it changes what every player is worth relative to MONEY.

THE CONFLICT AT THE HEART OF AN ANCHOR-ONLY CHANGE
========================================================================================

   from tools/wage-pyramid.mjs, ten seasons, real promotion and relegation:

   scale   worst D1 seat   clubs over $20m   ever in admin   wage/revenue
   -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    1.00     -$1,123,085                 0          77/256          60.2%
    0.90       -$529,425                 9          34/256          54.1%
    0.80        -$39,504                27          20/256          48.1%

   Phase 4 shipped a law that took clubs over $20m from three to ZERO and
   called that the flagship money printer being closed. An anchor deep
   enough to bring the worst Division One seat near break-even reopens it at
   twenty-seven clubs - nine times what Phase 4 removed. There is no scalar
   that closes the tail and leaves the top alone, because a scalar moves
   every club by the same proportion and the two problems sit at opposite
   ends of the same ladder.
